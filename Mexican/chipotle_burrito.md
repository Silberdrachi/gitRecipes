# Chipotle Burrito

## Ingredients
### Filling:
800g lean ground beef (5% or less) 
2 tsp smoked paprika
2 tsp garlic powder
1 tsp onion powder
1 tsp cayenne
1 tsp cumin
1 tsp oregano
2 tsp sea salt
1 tsp black pepper

### Chipotle sauce 
300g 0% fat cottage cheese
100g 0% fat Greek yoghurt
100g light mayo (brand Heinz lighter than light)
20g chipotle paste
1/2-1 lime juice
1 tbsp honey
1 tsp garlic powder
Salt to taste Other ingredients
100g grated mozzarella
100g reduced fat grated cheddar
Chopped cilantro (or parsley)

### Wrap
10 low carb tortillas
