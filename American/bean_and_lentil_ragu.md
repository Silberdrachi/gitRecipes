# Bean and Lentil Ragu with Pasta
https://www.youtube.com/shorts/ySFZQSDrtUc

## Ingredients
* 15g (1 Tbsp) olive oil
* 10 garlic cloves, grated
* 170g (1 small can) tomato paste
* 448g (16oz) 93% beef
* 1 tsp coarse salt
* 1 tsp black pepper
* 1 tsp red chili flakes
* 1 tsp Italian seasoning
* 425g canned lentils
* 425g (12oz.) plain can of cannelini beans
* 1 cup store bought vodka sauce
* 336g (12oz) Pasta
* 450ml (16oz) bone broth
* 336ml (12oz) water
* 120g (1/2 cup) whole milk skyr or Greek yogurt
* 30g parmigiano reggiano

## Preperation
1. Oil and garlic into a pot, let it sweat
1. Add tomato paste, stiring to combine
1. Add ground beef and salt, breaking it up and folding it into the ingredients. cook until the meat is browned and broken up
1. Add black pepper, red chili flakes, and italians sesasoning, mix to combine
1. Add lentils and cannelini beans, stiring to combine.
1. cover in broth, water, and add the vodka sauce
1. Add in pasta and stir to make sure everything is covered.
1. Set to a simmer, cover and wait 20 minutes stirring occasionally to make sure it doesnt burn.
1. Take off the heat and add your choice of yogurt or whole milk skyr, and parmigiano reggiano.
