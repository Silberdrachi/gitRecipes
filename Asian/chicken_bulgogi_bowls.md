# Chicken Bulgogi Bowls
https://www.reddit.com/r/MealPrepSunday/comments/1lzjczp/chicken_bulgogi_bowls/

## Ingredients
### Filling
* 1 lb ground chicken
* 3–4 cloves garlic, minced
* 1 tsp fresh ginger, grated (or ½ tsp ground)
* 5 green onions, chopped (reserve some green tops for garnish)
* 1 medium carrot, julienned (Japanese grater)
* 2 Tbsp low-sodium soy sauce
* 2 tsp mild gochujang (doubled for flavor)
* 1 tsp sesame oil
* 1 tsp rice vinegar
* 1 Tbsp monk fruit sweetener (or 1 tsp honey — adjust WWB points)
* 1 tsp oyster sauce

### Bowl Base (per serving)
* ⅔ cup cooked jasmine or brown rice (~4 WWB points)
* Thin layer of shredded Napa cabbage (optional, raw)
* Jumbo egg, poached in fine mesh sieve (see below)
* Toasted sesame seeds
* Green onion tops, thinly sliced
* Optional: Sriracha or gochujang drizzle

## Preperation
1. Whisk together soy sauce, gochujang, sesame oil, rice vinegar, monk fruit, and oyster sauce. Set aside.
1. In a large skillet over medium-high, cook ground chicken until lightly browned and crumbled.
1. Add garlic, ginger, chopped green onions, and julienned carrot. Cook 1–2 minutes.
1. Pour in sauce. Stir to coat everything evenly. Simmer 2–3 minutes until thickened slightly. If it looks dry at the end, splash in a tablespoon of water or rice vinegar.
1. In 5 containers, layer:
   * ⅔ cup cooked rice
   * Napa cabbage (if using)
   * Evenly divide bulgogi mixture
   * Top with sesame seeds and reserved green onion tops.
   * Add poached egg (see below) or keep it separate.
1. Let cool slightly before sealing.
   * Fridge: up to 4 days
   * Freezer: up to 2 months
   * Reheat from fridge: 1½–2 min
   * Reheat from frozen: 4–5 min (loosely covered)
