# Egg Roll Bowl
https://theproteinchef.co/healthy-egg-roll-in-a-bowl-recipe/

## Ingredients
* 454 g Ground Meat - Chicken, Beef, Turkey, or Pork
* 14 g Olive Oil
* 15 g Minced Garlic
* 150 g Sweet Onion - Chopped
* Black Pepper - For seasoning your meat
* 454 g Coleslaw Mix
* 113 g Carrots - Shredded
* 15 ml Rice Vinegar
* 14 g Sesame Oil
* 56 ml Soy Sauce - Low Sodium
* 2 g Ground Ginger
* 34 g Sweet & Sour Sauce - or Hoisin, Teriyaki, Oyster Sauce *Optional

## Preperation
1. Turn your burner on medium heat and add your Olive Oil into a large stovetop pan or skillet
1. Put your Ground Meat into your pan or skillet and brown it while doing your best to break it up as small as you can
1. As it's browning add in your Garlic, Onion, and Pepper
1. Once browned, turn your burner down to low heat
1. Add in the rest of your ingredients
1. Mix everything around and let it cook for 5-10 minutes or until it's as tender as you want it to be
1. Optionally add in some Green Onion, Sesame Seeds, Hot Sauce, or Hard Boiled Eggs!
